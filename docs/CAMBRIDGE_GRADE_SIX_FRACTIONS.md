# Cambridge Grade 6 — Calculation with Fractions

Source: attached Chapter 10, printed pages 152–165. No separate assessment pages appear in the attachment. Assessment adapts the four Let's Practise sections (pp. 155, 159, 162, 165).

| Objective | Interactive visual | Practice and assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Add and subtract unlike fractions | Equivalent strips with a common denominator | Estimate, calculate, compare with a whole, find a remainder |
| Use proper and improper fractions as operators | Divide a quantity into groups, then select a number of groups | Find a fraction of a quantity and reverse an operator |
| Multiply a proper fraction by a whole number | Reveal repeated equal strips | Estimate, calculate, and combine amounts before scaling |
| Divide a proper fraction by a whole number | Subdivide a strip and highlight one share | Estimate, share equally, correct an error, and divide in stages |
